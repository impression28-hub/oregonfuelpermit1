Extended weight permit

A flat-fee permit valid for one year — no mileage calculation needed. Choose highways only, or highways and county roads.

What is an extended weight permit?

An extended weight permit allows a qualifying vehicle combination to operate at gross weights above the standard limit for a full year, rather than for a single trip. It's a flat annual fee based on which roads you need to travel — highways only, or highways plus county roads.

Who typically needs one

  • Carriers running the same heavy configuration on a recurring basis
  • Fleets that need standing weight authority rather than a one-time trip permit
  • Vehicles that regularly use county roads in addition to state highways
