Frequently asked questions

Common questions about single-trip permits for Oregon, pricing, and the application process.

How much does a single-trip permit for Oregon cost?

Price depends on your vehicle's gross weight (and axle count for heavier combinations) and how many miles you'll drive in Oregon. You'll see the exact fee before you pay, once your route is calculated.

Can I use this for a round trip?

Yes. You can choose a one-way or round-trip permit when you build your route, and the mileage is calculated accordingly.

Do I need an exit point if I'm staying in Oregon?

No. If your trip starts or ends inside the state without crossing the border, you can leave the exit point blank and add your destination as a stop instead.

What information do I need before I start?

Your USDOT number, basic vehicle details (VIN, plate, weight), and your planned route — including where you'll enter and/or exit Oregon.

What's the difference between a trip permit and an extended weight permit?

A trip permit covers a single, specific trip in or through Oregon. An extended weight permit is for vehicle combinations that need to operate above standard weight limits on a more ongoing basis. See our Extended Weight Permit page for details.

Is my plate type going to affect my price?

Yes. Non-apportioned plates carry an additional flat fee on top of the mileage-based tax, in addition to the base permit fee.

How do I get help if I run into an issue?

Call us at (503) 862-6399 or email support@oregonfuelpermit.com and we'll help you sort it out.
