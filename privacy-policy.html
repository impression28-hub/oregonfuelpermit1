This Privacy Policy explains how Oregon Fuel Permit ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use this website to apply for a trip permit in Oregon or an extended weight permit.

Information we collect

  • Company information: USDOT number, company name, and phone number.
  • Contact information: your email address.
  • Vehicle and driver information: driver name, vehicle year/make, VIN, license plate details, weight, axle count, and cargo description.
  • Trip information: your entrance/exit points, stops, and route details.
  • Payment information: handled directly by our payment processor, Stripe. We do not store your full card number on our servers.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to calculate your permit fee, process your application, process payment, and send you confirmation of your permit. We may also use your contact information to respond to support requests.

Third-party services

We use third-party services to operate this site, including a routing/mapping provider to calculate distances and Stripe to process payments. These providers receive only the information necessary to perform their function (for example, route waypoints for distance calculation, or payment details for processing a charge).
